## Research Experience

### 1. HBV Genome Heterogeneity in Chronic Hepatitis B Patients

- High-resolution analysis of HBV quasispecies in chronic hepatitis B patients based on **targeted sequencing**.
- Detailed characterization of **intra-host genome heterogeneity, low-frequency functional variants, and their potential biological significance**.
- Integration of clinical samples and bioinformatics analysis to explore the relationship between viral diversity and **immune escape, antibody therapy response divergence**.
- Manuscript submitted to **GUT (IF 26.2)** — currently **under review**.

### 2. ADE Effects of SARS-CoV-2 Variants

- Investigated **antibody-dependent enhancement (ADE) differences** across SARS-CoV-2 variants.
- Integrated **self-generated Bulk RNA-seq** data with **scRNA-seq data from GEO/GSA databases** to systematically analyze host response differences induced by different viral strains.
- Characterized the impact of different variants on **PBMC proportion changes, cell phenotype remodeling, and immune feature alterations** in the ADE context.
- Established a research framework combining **in vitro functional assays + multi-omics data integration**.

### 3. Primer/Probe Design for Virus Detection Kit Development

- Conducted bioinformatics analysis based on public sequence data of common **adenoviruses** and **enteroviruses** (e.g., EV71, Coxsackie virus).
- Performed alignment, screening, and evaluation of **conserved regions** across different pathogens to design **primer/probe sequences** for detection.
- Supported third-party companies in developing virus colloidal gold detection kits based on **ERA technology**.
- Possess complete practical experience from **sequence collection, conserved region screening, candidate primer design to translational application support**.

### 4. Monoclonal Antibody Research

- Long-term engagement in **isolation, characterization, and functional profiling of fully human monoclonal antibodies**.
- Proficient in PBMC separation, flow cytometric sorting of antigen-specific single B cells, nested PCR amplification of antibody sequences, antibody expression and purification.
- Capable of independently conducting antibody binding activity assays, neutralization assays, ADE-related detection, and in vivo functional evaluation.

### 5. Early Research Training

- Participated in **miRNA biomarker screening for electrocution-related death** research at Fudan University's forensic medicine department.
- Possess interdisciplinary research experience, able to apply experimental techniques and data analysis methods to diverse biomedical questions.
