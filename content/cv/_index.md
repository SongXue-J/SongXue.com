<h2 class="cv-section-title">Research Interests</h2>
<p class="cv-interests">Pathogen biology and humoral immunity, focusing on antibody discovery, screening, and functional characterization. Research integrates high-throughput single B cell screening, deep sequencing, and single-cell multi-omics to study how antibody sequence and structure influence broad neutralizing activity and antibody-dependent enhancement (ADE).</p>

<h2 class="cv-section-title">Research Experience</h2>
<div class="cv-project">
  <p class="cv-role">High-Throughput Screening and Functional Validation of Broad-Spectrum Anti-SARS-CoV-2 Neutralizing Antibodies</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Developed and optimized a high-throughput affinity evaluation platform for multiplexed antigen–antibody screening based on 10x single-cell transcriptome–BCR–surface antigen co-sequencing (LIBRA-seq).</li>
    <li>Identified multiple monoclonal antibodies with ultra-broad binding to the receptor-binding domains of diverse coronaviruses.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
<div class="cv-project">
  <p class="cv-role">Epitope-Specific Mechanisms of Antibody-Dependent Enhancement (ADE) Across SARS-CoV-2 Variants</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Used structurally characterized monoclonal antibodies to investigate how epitope specificity drives distinct ADE phenotypes.</li>
    <li>Integrated bulk RNA-seq and scRNA-seq data to compare ADE effects and host immune responses across SARS-CoV-2 variants.</li>
    <li>Compared the ADE effects of antibodies from COVID-19 vaccine recipients and convalescent individuals.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
<div class="cv-project">
  <p class="cv-role">Isolation and Identification of Fully Human Monoclonal Antibodies Targeting HBV Core/e Antigens</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Established and optimized a prokaryotic expression system for the in vitro recombinant production of HBV core and e antigens.</li>
    <li>Isolated multiple fully human monoclonal antibodies from HBV-convalescent individuals that efficiently recognize both antigens.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
<div class="cv-project">
  <p class="cv-role">High-Resolution Profiling of HBV Quasispecies and Immune Escape in Chronic Hepatitis B</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Applied MutPE-seq, a high-fidelity paired-end deep sequencing strategy, to improve the detection resolution of low-frequency HBV variants to 0.5%.</li>
    <li>Profiled 35 patients with chronic hepatitis B at high resolution, linking low-frequency mutations and genomic heterogeneity to clinical phenotype, viral replication fitness, and antibody therapy response.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
