## About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Pathogen Biology at **Fudan University** (2022–present), focused on novel strategies and technologies for the prevention and treatment of emerging infectious diseases. I received my B.Sc. in Forensic Medicine from **Fudan University** (2017–2022).

{{< callout emoji="🔬" >}}
**Core Research** — Antibody-dependent enhancement (ADE) of SARS-CoV-2 variants, HBV genome heterogeneity in chronic hepatitis B, and high-throughput screening of broadly neutralizing antibodies.
{{< /callout >}}
