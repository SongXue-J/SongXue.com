I am a Ph.D. student studying **pathogens and humoral immunity**. My research bridges wet-lab molecular experiments with computational analysis, focusing on antibody responses against viral pathogens.

## Research focus

- **Humoral immunity** — B cell and antibody responses to viral infection and vaccination
- **High-throughput single-cell sequencing** — TCR/BCR repertoire profiling, transcriptomics
- **Antibody characterization** — binding affinity, neutralization, epitope mapping

## What this blog covers

This blog documents my journey navigating the intersection of bench work and computational analysis. You'll find:

- **R data visualization & processing** — practical techniques for biological data
- **Single-cell sequencing** — pipeline walkthroughs, paper reproductions, best practices
- **Research software tips** — FlowJo, Chimera, PyMOL, Schrödinger, and more
- **AI agent workflows** — building and integrating LLM agents into research workflows
